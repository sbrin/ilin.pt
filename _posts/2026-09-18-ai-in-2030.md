**2030**

artificial intelligence enters the social welfare system and is provided as a basic public service

it's available the same way electricity and the internet are, costs next to nothing, and is mostly subsidized

it's a sanitized, licensed, and approved version

it exists strictly within the limits described by law

all artificial intelligence is monopolized by corporations and the state

access to AI is strictly controlled by law

it's forbidden to develop and host models yourself without a license

there are strict limits and hard requirements on model parameters and sizes available to ordinary people

anything more powerful than today's deepseek 4 is banned for personal use

today's frontier models are just as illegal as the darknet, viruses and other malware, or restricted radio frequencies

research in artificial intelligence and the development of new models are also strictly limited

it's regulated at roughly the level of bioweapons or plutonium

the usa is bombing iran because they supposedly are building data centers and developing ai there

What else is coming?
