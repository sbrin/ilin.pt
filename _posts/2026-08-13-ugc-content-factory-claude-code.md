Even though I've been quiet about it for a long time, I'm still grinding away on Lopaka. And the biggest problem and growth lever we've identified right now is videos on Instagram and TikTok.

So far it looks like the views on our videos correlate with the amount of traffic and the number of sales.

So for the last six months I've been looking for any way to make my life easier and scale this process. One of the options was suggested by Maxim [@startupfomo](https://t.me/startupfomo), who does this professionally. But the most important thing is that he put together a cool [guide](https://boont.ai/guide) on how to do it.

And even with a step-by-step guide, nothing worked using the methods I'm used to. I struggled for a long time and couldn't get anywhere near our UGC. I'd look at these presentation slides, see what needed to be done, and just freeze up.

The very first step, doing research and collecting some references, took the two of us about a month!!! That's just gathering viral videos in our niche that could potentially be adapted for Lopaka.

**And the ice only broke when I started treating this project like vibe coding.**

Instead of digging into every step myself and trying to fill in and write out everything, I made a folder and asked the agent to turn this guide into a full step-by-step playbook. And together with the agent I started filling it in. In another project I already have all of Lopaka's context, so we pull some of the answers from there.

So, bit by bit: here I hooked up an API, there I plugged in some data, somewhere I answered the agent's questions, helped it out, and the work got moving. A separate file gets created for every step and every piece of knowledge. And the project info is slowly getting collected in different places too, everything's written down.

It turned out that following a ready-made guide that the agent just put together is way easier than staring at this huge pile of information that you have to sort through and put together.

Anyway, we're building a content factory... well, trying to.

I'll keep you posted on how it goes.
