I'm part of [SLP](https://startupleadership.ru/community), one of the liveliest communities of Russian-speaking entrepreneurs I know.

Two years ago I quit my job to work on my own projects as an indie hacker. And a year ago I made a tough decision for myself and applied to SLP.

Actually, my first contact with the program was about three years ago. Back then it seemed expensive and confusing to me, but it was really intriguing.

The program was recommended by a lot of cool dudes I've been following on Telegram for a long time: [@Matskevich](https://t.me/Matskevich), [@zamesin](https://t.me/zamesin), [@vsevolodustinovchannel](https://t.me/vsevolodustinovchannel), and others, too many to list.

And so a year ago I closed my accounts in Russia and sold off what was left of my securities to pay the SLP fee. And that's not cheap at all, and it was a really tough decision. But, as they say, listen to your heart. I took a leap of faith, and a year later I don't regret that decision one bit.

Right now [enrollment for the SLP program](https://startupleadership.ru/program) is open. It's a peer-to-peer education program for entrepreneurs and the only way to join the community. I want to recommend that you take part.

The program itself is just the entry point. The goal isn't to teach you something. The most important thing I got was a ton of support, new connections, a big network. And, just as important, I got rid of the feeling of loneliness.

Being surrounded by a high concentration of active, upbeat people who share the same values gives me something to lean on every day. That's especially missing when you're an emigrant. And I also found a co-founder here, and together we're growing the B2B side of Lopaka, my main project.

Cohorts only run once a year and only take 30 people, so if this speaks to you, I'd advise not putting it off and [applying](https://t.me/slp_russia_bot)!
