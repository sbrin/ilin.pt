I've realized that jokes about Jensen Huang are unethical. You can't just take a person and turn them into memes and make fun of the situation.

Imagine a close friend or a relative of yours in his place.

And the spreading of these jokes and memes should stop.

It's one thing when everyone's discussing the news, and a completely different thing when a bunch of deepfakes and gags of varying degrees of cruelty are pouring out of every corner. It's sad.
