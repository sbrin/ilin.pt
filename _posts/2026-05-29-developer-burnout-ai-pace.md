This post is for programmers, developers, and other IT folks of advanced age (30+)

The world has changed. It changes every day. I stopped writing code by hand. And one of OpenAI's founders stopped writing code by hand all the way back six months ago.

We haven't started working less, though. The operations we perform have changed. The rhythm of the work has changed.

We used to have a standup, then we'd analyze the task, then look for where in the code we needed to change something, then write the code, then check how it turned out, then deploy it or ship it somewhere or whatever.

There was plenty of time to think, twiddle your thumbs, or just peck away at the keys by hand. And just like that, the week was winding down.

A pretty calm, predictable rhythm and a long feedback loop. A release might come once every two weeks or once a month.

And now? The same cycle, sped up 10x. Except you don't have to dig through the source code anymore. You don't have to type code anymore. Testing and deployment got way simpler.

And what's left? Nonstop analysis, design, and evaluating the results. A huge density of brainwork that's no longer diluted by routine or typing on a keyboard. You have to make decisions constantly. It makes for a super intense workday.

Short feedback loops add to the fun and fry your reward system like nothing else.

For me as a solo founder, there's an extra loop on top: releases and social media announcements. And every release is stress, every social media post is stress. The release frequency went up, so the stress frequency went up.

I think the number of people burning out is only going to keep growing over the next few years.

And this shit is inevitable: now that I have super-fast tools that replace a whole pile of jobs, I **don't want** to do it any other way anymore. It's just not fun.

Besides, everyone around me has sped up, and I simply **can't** do it any other way anymore, because then my product would fall behind the market.

Hell, I'm at risk myself as an employee, because the new generations already come with this baseline firmware. Vibe coding is the new normal.

I only dug this thing up at my therapist's today, and I was totally floored. Take care of yourselves. Practice good work hygiene. Take breaks. Drink some water.

Work didn't get easier. It got different. And I wasn't ready for that.
